About K2 Capital

A student-run club at Queen's University bridging the gap between accounting and finance.

Our mission

Accounting and finance are often taught as separate worlds, but great investors read both fluently. K2 Capital brings students from both sides together to analyze real companies: reading financial statements, building valuation models, and putting those ideas to work in a live club portfolio.
